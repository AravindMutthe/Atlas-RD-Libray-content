# Atlas primary-source recovery export: 2025-08-01 through 2026-01-31

This is a DATA EXPORT of **recoverable historical evidence**, not merely a topic index. It is **not** a complete export of all ChatGPT conversations. The history lookup returned dated fragments and summaries, not all original message bodies. No content has been invented to fill missing transcript gaps.

## Files
- HISTORICAL_EVIDENCE.csv / .json: dated evidence with source type, content, Atlas mapping and limitations.
- PROJECT_DATA.json: recoverable historical project and repository details.
- ATLAS_INTEGRATION.html: navigable research data and cross-links by topic.
- FULL_ATLAS_OFFLINE.html: existing Atlas baseline (included without replacing its original notes).

## Date coverage
August 2025: partial. September 2025: substantial selected project evidence. October 2025: partial. November 2025: partial. December 2025 and January 2026: **no full original conversation transcripts recovered**.

## Provenance policy
'user statement summary' = history system's summary of an original user statement; 'assistant-summary evidence' = history system's summary of an earlier assistant response; 'email-derived summary' = summarized project correspondence, not full raw emails. None of these labels mean verbatim transcript.

## What is needed for exhaustive recovery
Export ChatGPT account data (Settings → Data Controls → Export Data, if available), then provide the exported conversations archive. A complete account export can be processed chronologically, retaining original messages, project titles and timestamps, deduplicating and mapping relevant entries to Atlas chapters. Other app/project repositories require their own exports or snapshots.

## Privacy and authorship
Historical personal employment, immigration, medical and unrelated private details are excluded from the public-facing technical narrative. The files are local exports only; no external publication is performed. Original handwritten source material is unchanged in the inherited Atlas.
